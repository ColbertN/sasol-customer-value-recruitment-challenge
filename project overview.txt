Every transaction tells a story - but can it also reveal what a customer will do next?

Sasol’s loyalty data captures how customers spend across fuel and convenience retail. Hidden within those transaction histories are signals about future customer value: who is likely to keep spending, who may become inactive, and where there may be opportunities for cross-sell or growth. This challenge asks you to turn those signals into forward-looking predictions that can help Sasol better understand and prioritise its customers.

You will build models to tackle two linked tasks: predict each customer’s fuel volume in litres and non-fuel spend in rands over the next three months, and predict their most likely future behaviour - from inactivity risk to new-category adoption, existing-category growth, or stable activity.

Evaluation
This challenge uses multi-metric evaluation. There are two evaluation metrics: RMSE and F1 Score. CLV_fuel and CLV_nonfuel are scored separately using standard RMSE on log-transformed, normalised targets. Opportunity is scored using Weighted F1 on the combined opportunity type-and-category labels, including the standalone Inactivity and Stable labels.

For each regression target, transform the raw three-month customer total y as z = ln(1 + y) / s, where ln is the natural logarithm and s is the population standard deviation of ln(1 + y) calculated from a fixed historical calibration set, separately for fuel and non-fuel. Raw fuel totals are in litres and raw non-fuel totals are in rands. The scaling constants and calibration definition will be published with the label-generation rules and remain fixed for all submissions. Only historical outcomes before the test cutoff are used to calculate these constants.

Submit the transformed values z in CLV_fuel and CLV_nonfuel. Standard RMSE is calculated directly between submitted and true transformed values, without applying the transformation a second time. A raw prediction can be recovered using y = exp(s × z) − 1. Zero raw totals transform to zero.

The final leaderboard score is:

Score = 0.3 × RMSE CLV_fuel + 0.3 × RMSE CLV_nonfuel + 0.4 × Weighted F1 Opportunity.
